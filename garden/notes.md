## Finishing Vs Perfection
type: concept
source: World
tags: process, creativity
The tension I keep running into with my projects: when does finishing something matter more than perfecting it? Ask yourself, what is more important to me in this moment, the perfection of what I am creating or the completion of this project and my ability to create more? When does sharing unfinished work become more important than wrapping something up neatly? When does the project matter more than the finishing? Each time you find yourself at this crossroads, these and more questions are important to ask yourself. If you are going to continue striving for completion, perfection, etc, the why must be worth it.

## Children of Time Series
type: source
source: Books
tags: book, sci-fi
This series by Adrian Tchaikovsky explores alien evolution, how different species might evolve into intelligent beings. His work is some of the most captivating imaginings of hypotheticals I have ever read. The concept is incredibly unique and it is clear he has a background in zoology. I am really fond of media that goes into the human condition, and this series expands on that exponentially. What is the nature of life?
